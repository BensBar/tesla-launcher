Help

Signing in and accounts

Each button just opens the official website in this tab. Sign in on that website and pick the right account there. YouTube and YouTube TV use different Google accounts for you, so you may need to switch accounts inside each service.

This launcher never sees or stores passwords, cookies, tokens or email addresses, and it cannot control another site's login. Separate buttons do not keep separate sessions. Whether each service remembers its account in the car must be tested in the car.

Fullscreen

Browser fullscreen (the Fullscreen button, when shown) only affects this page. It usually ends when you open a service.

Tesla Theater fullscreen is a different, Tesla-controlled mode. No official way to launch it from a web page has been found, so it is unverified. The optional experimental method in Settings is community-reported only.

Good to know

Your list is saved only in this browser. The Tesla browser keeps its own list, separate from your phone or computer.

Settings

This browser is blocking local storage, so changes will be lost when you leave this page. The default links still work.
